# MentorOS FAQ

## Q: What is MentorOS?
MentorOS is an AI-powered smart mentoring platform designed for engineering institutes. It helps faculty mentors track student progress, log meetings, and get AI-assisted insights.

## Q: Who can use MentorOS?
MentorOS has three types of users: students, mentors (faculty), and administrators (HOD/admin). Each role has a dedicated dashboard with different capabilities.

## Q: How does mentor allocation work?
Mentors are allocated to students based on department and availability. Each mentor can be assigned up to a fixed number of students, configured by the admin.

## Q: How do I log a meeting with my mentor?
Mentors can log meetings from their dashboard by clicking "Log Meeting", selecting the student, adding the date, and writing session notes. The meeting is then visible on the student's timeline.

## Q: What is the AI companion in MentorOS?
The AI companion is a RAG-powered chatbot that answers questions about MentorOS features, policies, and mentoring guidance based on the institute's knowledge base.
